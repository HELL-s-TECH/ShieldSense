How it works

How ShieldSense decides

A tiered pipeline — fast where it can be, careful where it has to be.

1

Watch

ShieldSense checks every new email, link, or file the moment it shows up — you don't have to send it anything yourself.

2

Fast filter

It first looks for obvious red flags, like a fake sender address or a suspicious link. Most items get cleared right here, instantly.

3

Deep reasoning

For anything unclear, it compares the content to scams it has learned from, then reasons through the details and writes out what it found in plain words.

4

Guarded action

It never blocks or deletes anything on its own — you always get the final say, and every check is saved so you can look back later.

Why ShieldSense

Not just another spam filter

Signature scanners look things up. ShieldSense reasons about them.

Reasons, doesn't just match

Signature lists miss anything new. ShieldSense reasons about intent and behavior, catching disguised and never-seen-before attacks.

Explains itself

Every alert is a specific, plain-language explanation — not a red "Danger!" label you learn to ignore.

Never acts alone

Blocking and quarantining always wait for your confirmation. The agent recommends; you decide.

Keeps a paper trail

Every scan, automatic or manual, is logged — full accountability, not a black box.

Reaches you anywhere

High-risk alerts also go out by SMS, so you're covered even when you're not staring at the inbox.
